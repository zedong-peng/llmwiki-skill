---
name: llmwiki-query
description: Answer questions from a personal Markdown wiki without modifying it, using summaries, notes, or archived originals as needed.
---

Locate the wiki from the request or workspace; clarify only if the target remains
ambiguous. Follow its AGENTS.md. [Navigation](references/navigation.md) describes
the layout; choose index, notes, or originals to fit the question.

Cite the evidence used and distinguish source claims, observations, and interpretation.
State evidence gaps; use `llmwiki-collect` when the user requests ingestion or validation.

Keep wiki files unchanged. Local search and parsing tools are allowed; do not execute
downloaded research code or its install/build scripts without authorization, or
upload private material without authorization.
